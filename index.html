How to Choose a Virtual Card for Ad Spend: A Checklist

Ad platforms are strict about cards. A payment that works for a subscription can be declined by Google Ads or Meta, and a card that runs out mid-campaign pauses your ads. This checklist covers what to compare before you pick a virtual card for advertising, whichever provider you use.

1. Where the card is issued

Platforms check the card's issuing country. A card issued in the same country as the platform's billing entity (often the US) avoids cross-border fees and is refused less often by platforms that are wary of foreign cards. Our guide to virtual cards with a US billing address explains why the address on the card matters.

2. The total cost, not the headline fee

  • Card fee and loading (top-up) fee
  • Monthly fee per card, if any
  • Cross-border or FX fee when the merchant is in another country (often 1.5-3%)
  • Fees on declined payments: some providers charge per decline, and ad platforms retry failed charges

3. One card per ad account

Use a separate card for each ad account, client or campaign. A decline, a dispute or a frozen card then touches only that account, and spend per client is easy to track. See how to use virtual cards for ad spend.

4. Keep the card funded above the next charge

Google Ads and Meta charge at billing thresholds and retry when a charge fails. Keep more on the card than your next expected charge, so a retry never hits an empty card. Platform-specific tips: paying for Google Ads and paying for Facebook Ads.

5. 3D Secure and billing address

Some charges ask for a one-time code (3D Secure). Make sure your provider supports it and shows you the code. At checkout, use the card's own billing address, not your home address; a mismatch is a common reason for declines. More in why a card is declined online.

6. Getting your money back

Check whether you can close a card and receive the remaining balance back, how fast, and at what cost. A card you can't empty is money locked away.

7. Support when a payment fails

When an ad payment fails, you need someone who can see why: a decline reason, a held payment, a 3D Secure timeout. Test support before you move a large budget.
